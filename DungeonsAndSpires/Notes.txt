Dungeons and Spires: a mod that takes DND and turns it into a card game for Slay the spire.

Core features (WIP)
    - Save DC. a core feature in dnd is the saving throws for effects. since sts2, rng on damage shouldnt be a thing. i have to come up with a way to scale the player through a combat
    perhaps, when in dnd a save is needed. in this sts2 mod, your "ability save DC" can increase (like focus/strength) and increase the damage you deal with ability damage.
    THIS IS NOT NAMED 'Ability Potency'
    - subclasses: a big part of dnd is customization, i think, adding a way to 'subclass' is a fun idea. after defeating a boss for example, you can choose boons to develop the character
    - for character core mechanics, a lot dnd classes have 2 core mechanics and a subclass. idk how to do that yet. i think the best way is to let the player choose their starting subclass as their "3rd archetype" 
    - reactions: i think these will be relegated to powers... things like 'spell: shield' can be a "when hit, gain 5 block". 
    - Spells: What i think i want to do for spells, is that spell cards can be played with energy, but are free to play when you have a spell slot of the level available
    with other cards or effects, spell slots can be regained in combat. characters gain status effects when casting without a spellslot. 
    This status effect is [Arcane Exhaustion], this is an unplayable, ethereal card that lowers potency by its lvl amount.
    not every character has access to spell slots, but martial classes also dont really get spell cards, unless theyre some subclass that allows it.
    Max Spellslots naturally increase as you play

Characters:
    Sorcerer:
    - a magic caster, [CORE]: spell slots, MetaMagic (points),  [Draconic bloodline, wild magic, Lunar sorcerer]
    - [Spell slots]: see 'Spells'
    - [MetaMagic]: Sorcery points can be spend when casting spells, for example, twin spell can turn a firebolt (single target) to a (target all). 
    or (heigthened spell) can be used to increase the damage of spell that deal Ability damage. these points can be gained from card effects and a potion or two.
    - [Draconic bloodline]: (act1) increased max HP, (act2) strength is twice as effective on single target, ability damage, (act3) gain 1 "fly anwhere" after defeating an elite
    - [Wild Magic]: (act1) when a spellslot reaches 0 uses, put a random spellcard from your draw pile in your hand, 
    (act2) ability potency is increased by the amount of status cards in all your piles, (act3) when you generate a status effect card, gain 1 energy. 
    - [Lunar Sorcerer]: (act1) once per combat, when metamagic reaches 0, gain 2 energy. (act2) heal 5hp when the last kill in an encounter is from spell damage. 
    (act3) playing a scrolled spell or a spell affected by your metamagic draw 1 card.
